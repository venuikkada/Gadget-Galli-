# How to sell Gadget Galli: sales & marketing playbook

This is a practical guide for the founder and the first team. It covers how to sign up shops, how to get customers, what to say, where to spend, and which numbers to watch. Everything here works with features already in the app and admin panel, which are named in **bold**.

---

## The 12 things that matter most

1. **Shops before customers, and density before reach.** Ten great shops in *one* cluster beat fifty scattered across the city. Customers come back only when search finds the item **and** a shop that delivers to them.
2. **Start where people already buy electronics:** Kukatpally/KPHB, Chenoy Trade Centre (CTC), Ameerpet, Koti/Abids, Dilsukhnagar, Madhapur/Hitech City.
3. **Make "fast, local, real" the promise:** *"Hyderabad's electronics shops, delivered in hours. Compare prices, talk to the shop, pay the shop."*
4. **Never charge shops at the start.** Use a **Founding Partner** offer: 0% fees for 6 months, a free QR poster, and listing done for them.
5. **Every shop is a marketing channel.** The **printable QR poster** goes on the counter, the shop link goes in their WhatsApp Business profile, and a broadcast goes to their customer list. That alone brings your first few thousand users.
6. **Shops must reply in minutes.** The **loud new-order alert** exists for this. Track confirm time weekly and coach slow shops. Slow replies kill the app faster than anything else.
7. **Use what people search for.** Check **Admin → Reports → Searches with no results** and the shops' **Demand near you** every week. It tells you which products and shops to add next.
8. **Short videos sell.** "RTX 4060 price in Hyderabad today: 4 shops compared" type Reels and Shorts, in Telugu and English, 4–5 a week.
9. **WhatsApp is the main channel:** apartment groups, college groups, office groups, gamer groups. Use the in-app **share links** and **referral codes**.
10. **CCTV is the sleeper hit.** Every gated community, clinic and kirana store needs it. Bundle the kit with same-day installation.
11. **Trust is the product.** Give the **Verified** badge only after a visit. Push reviews, show "pay only after the shop confirms", and solve problems within 24 hours.
12. **Measure one north-star number: delivered orders per week.** Then fix the biggest drop in the funnel each week (see §13).

## 3. Supply: signing up shops (start here)

### 3.1 Targets

| By | Live shops | Listings | Coverage |

### 3.2 The field visit (20–25 minutes per shop)

Go in pairs, after 11 AM and before 1 PM or between 3 and 6 PM. Avoid rush hours and Sundays. Carry a demo phone with the app logged in as a customer, sample QR posters and printed one-page brochures.

**The 2-minute pitch (English):**
> "Namaste! I'm from Gadget Galli, a Hyderabad app where people search for electronics and see which **nearby shops** have it and at what price. When someone in Kukatpally searches 'RTX 4060', your shop shows up with your price. They **call or WhatsApp you directly**, and pay **straight to your UPI**. We never touch the money.
> It's **free for 6 months**. No commission. We'll list your top products for you today and give you a QR poster for your counter. Shall I show you on my phone how a customer sees your shop?"

**Telugu (key lines):**
> "మీ ఏరియాలో ఎవరైనా ఫోన్‌లో ప్రొడక్ట్ వెతికితే, మీ షాప్, మీ ధరతో కనిపిస్తుంది. వాళ్లు మీకే నేరుగా కాల్ లేదా WhatsApp చేసి ఆర్డర్ ఇస్తారు. డబ్బు నేరుగా మీ UPIకే వస్తుంది. 6 నెలలు పూర్తిగా ఫ్రీ, కమిషన్ లేదు."

**Hindi (key lines):**
> "आपके इलाके में कोई भी फ़ोन पर प्रोडक्ट खोजेगा तो आपकी दुकान आपके दाम के साथ दिखेगी। ग्राहक सीधे आपको कॉल या WhatsApp करके ऑर्डर देगा, और पैसा सीधे आपके UPI में आएगा। 6 महीने बिल्कुल फ़्री, कोई कमीशन नहीं।"

*(Have a native speaker polish these lines and record them as a 30-second voice note for the team.)*

**Show, don't tell.** On the demo phone:
1. Search their best-selling item in their area.
2. Show the product page with "Available at these shops".
3. Show a shop page with reviews.
4. Place a test order to the demo shop and let them hear the **loud new-order ring**.

Owners remember the sound.

### 3.3 Objections and answers

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "What's the commission?" | "Zero for 6 months. Customers pay you directly by UPI. After that, a small fixed monthly plan only if it's working for you." |
| "I already sell on WhatsApp / Instagram." | "Great, keep doing that. We bring **new** customers who don't know you yet. Your link and QR also make your WhatsApp selling easier." |
| "I don't have time to list products." | "We'll do it now, in 15 minutes, from our catalog. You just tell us your price. Later you can update prices with one tap or upload an Excel sheet." |
| "Online prices are lower." | "Customers here want it **today**, with local warranty and someone to call. You can see in the app what nearby shops charge. Price smartly; you don't need to be cheapest." |
| "Delivery is a problem." | "Use Porter, Rapido or Uber. The app has shortcuts to open them, or offer store pickup. You set your own delivery areas and charges." |
| "What about fake orders?" | "Every customer logs in with their phone OTP. You talk to them before confirming, and they pay before you dispatch." |
| "Is my data / document safe?" | "Documents are visible only to the Gadget Galli team for verification. Customers never see them." |
| "Big shops will get all the orders." | "Search shows price, distance, delivery time and rating, so fast, friendly shops win. Reviews matter more than size." |

### 3.4 The Founding Partner offer (first 100 shops)
- **0% fees for 6 months**, written on the brochure.
- **Free printed QR poster and counter standee** (the app's **My shop link & QR → Download printable poster** creates the A4 PDF in English, Telugu and Hindi).
- **We list your first 50 products** using **bulk upload** (CSV) or the catalog.
- **"Founding Partner" featured placement** on the home screen for 3 months (**Admin → Content → Featured**).
- **Verified badge** after a quick visit check (**Admin → Shops → Trust badges**).
- **Free product photo session** for their top 10 items (phone photos in daylight are enough).

### 3.5 In-shop onboarding checklist (do it together, on their phone)
1. Install the app and log in with the owner's phone. Choose **"I own a shop"**.
2. Complete the 8-step wizard: hours, contacts, map pin, **delivery areas** (start small: 5–8 km), shop-front photo, **UPI ID + QR photo**, documents. Then **Submit**.
3. Add 20–50 top products with real prices and stock.
4. Turn on notifications and **disable battery saver** (the dashboard shows a tip). Place a test order from your phone so the owner hears the alert.
5. Print the poster, stick it at the billing counter, and send the shop link to the owner's top WhatsApp contacts and broadcast lists.
6. Add them to the **"Gadget Galli Partners" WhatsApp community** for tips and support.

The admin team then reviews and approves the shop within 24 hours (**Admin → Shops → To review**).

### 3.6 The first 30 days: make every shop successful
- **Day 1:** call to confirm they're live and explain the order steps (Accept → Payment received → Packed → Send).
- **Day 3:** share their **Demand near you** list ("people near you searched for RTX 4070 Super 23 times this week") and help them add those items.
- **Weekly:** a 5-minute call covering views, call and WhatsApp taps and orders (their **Insights** tab), confirm time, reviews to reply to and prices to update.
- **Shop rules of thumb:**
  - Confirm within **5 minutes**.
  - Keep stock accurate. A "sorry, out of stock" after a customer calls hurts everyone.
  - Use real photos.
  - Give GST bills when possible.
  - Reply to every review.
- **Three strikes:** three unhandled requests or genuine complaints in a month means a warning (**Admin → Shops → Warn**). Further problems mean suspension. Protect the customer experience ruthlessly.

## 4. Demand: getting customers

Ranked from cheapest and most effective to most expensive.

### 4.1 Your shops' own customers (free, start on day 1)
Each shop already has hundreds of customers in its phone. Help every shop:
- Stick the **QR poster** at the counter and billing desk.
- Send a **WhatsApp broadcast**, ready to paste:
  > "Namaste! You can now see our full stock and prices on the **Gadget Galli** app and order on WhatsApp or call. Delivered in hours across [area]. Our shop: [link]. Save it for next time! 🙏"
- Add the **shop link** to their WhatsApp Business profile, Google Business listing and Instagram bio.

Target: **20–50 customers per shop** in the first month, or 1,000–2,500 customers from 50 shops at ₹0 acquisition cost.

### 4.2 WhatsApp groups and communities
- **Apartment associations** (CCTV, routers, UPS): one message per community, through a resident or the association secretary:
  > "🔒 CCTV for our community/homes: compare 4-camera kits from verified Hyderabad shops, **installed the same day**. Pay the shop only after they confirm. [link to CCTV kits]"
- **College, office and gamer groups:** share specific product links. The **share button** on every product creates a link that opens the app.
- **Status updates:** have the team and ambassadors post a "deal of the day" product link on their WhatsApp Status every day.
- **Rule:** never spam. One useful message per group per fortnight, ideally posted by a member.

### 4.3 Short videos (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts), the main paid-media engine
Post **4–5 per week**: about 60% Telugu or Tenglish, 40% English or Hindi. Content pillars and ready ideas:
1. **Price checks:** "RTX 4060 price in Hyderabad TODAY, 4 shops compared on Gadget Galli". Show the product page with the shop list, then call a shop live.
2. **Speed challenge:** "Ordered a phone at 7:10 PM, delivered at 8:55 PM. Kukatpally." Show the timeline screen.
3. **Shop owner stories:** "20 years in CTC: meet Srinivas garu of KPHB Computer World". Build trust and make shop owners proud to share.
4. **Explainers:** "4-camera vs 8-camera CCTV kit: what does your house need?" and "Best GPU under ₹30,000 in Hyderabad".
5. **Unboxing at the counter:** real shop, real stock, with the GST bill visible.
6. **CCTV installed same day:** a time-lapse of the install at a customer's house (with permission).
7. **Myths:** "Are local shops more expensive than online? We checked 10 products." Be honest; sometimes online is cheaper. Honesty builds the brand.
8. **Festival hooks** (see §9).

Format: hook in the first 2 seconds, on-screen text in Telugu and English, end card "Search on Gadget Galli: link in bio". Boost the best 20% of posts with ₹500–2,000 each, targeted within 8–10 km of your live clusters.

### 4.4 Google (people searching right now)
- **Google Business Profile** for "Gadget Galli": category "Electronics store / online marketplace", with photos, posts and Q&A.
- **Google Ads, search only**, on high-intent phrases near live clusters:
  - `rtx 4060 price hyderabad`, `graphics card shop near me`, `cctv installation kukatpally`, `cctv camera price hyderabad`, `iphone 15 price hyderabad`, `laptop shop madhapur`, `phone shop near me open now`.
  - Expect ₹10–40 per click. Start with ₹500–1,000 per day and keep the keywords that bring orders (track with UTM links).
- **SEO (build next):** a public web page per product and area ("RTX 4060 price in Kukatpally: 3 shops, from ₹29,099") generated from the catalog. These pages can rank for thousands of "price in Hyderabad" searches for free. They are the best long-term growth asset (see §15).

### 4.5 Meta ads (Instagram + Facebook)
- Geo-target **5–8 km radius** around each live cluster.
- Use separate creatives per segment (gamers, CCTV for homes, phones today).
- Optimise for **app installs**, then for "order requested" once you have 50+ conversion events.
- Budget: ₹30,000–1,00,000 per month at first.
- Kill any ad set with cost per first order above ₹250.

### 4.6 Communities: PC builders and gamers
- Be genuinely useful in r/hyderabad, r/IndianGaming, Hyderabad PC-builder Discord and Telegram groups. Answer "where to buy X in Hyderabad" questions with real comparisons.
- Partner with 3–5 **gaming cafés** for a poster and a monthly "build of the month" giveaway with a partner shop.
- Sponsor small college esports tournaments (₹5–10k) in exchange for logo placement and a discount code.

### 4.7 Campus ambassadors (from July for back-to-college)
- 1–2 ambassadors per college (JNTU-H, OU, IIIT-H, CBIT, VNR VJIET, Vasavi, MGIT, BVRIT, CVR, Gokaraju and others).
- Pay **₹100 per friend's first delivered order** (tracked through their **referral code**, **Admin → Content → Referrals**) plus a ₹2,000 monthly bonus at 25+ orders.
- Run fest stalls with a "check any laptop price live" desk.

### 4.8 IT corridor
- "WFH setup in 2 hours" bundles: monitor, keyboard and mouse, webcam, UPS. Shops in Madhapur, Kondapur and Gachibowli can bundle-price these.
- HR and admin tie-ups: "new joiner laptop accessories" and festive gifting for companies.
- Posters in tech-park food courts and residential towers near Hitec City (with permission).

### 4.9 CCTV: a dedicated play
- Partner with CCTV shops on a **free site survey**: the shop calls within an hour and gives a quote on WhatsApp.
- Messages for apartments and businesses: "secure before you travel" (summer and festival holidays) and "monitor your shop from your phone".
- Bundles: 4-camera kit + 1 TB HDD + installation; video door phone + installation.
- Push campaign to the **Area** segment for neighbourhoods where CCTV shops deliver (**Admin → Content → Push campaigns**).

### 4.10 Offline and PR (after you have density)
- QR standees at every partner shop (already done in §3).
- Auto-rickshaw back panels near clusters (₹800–1,500 per auto per month), with a big QR code.
- Metro station posters at Ameerpet, JNTU College and Hitec City once there are 100+ shops.
- Local PR: Telangana Today, Deccan Chronicle, The Hindu (Hyderabad), Eenadu and Sakshi (Telugu), YourStory and the T-Hub network. Story angle: *"Hyderabad startup puts 150 local electronics shops online, delivers in hours, never touches customers' money."*

## 5. Growth loops built into the app (use them deliberately)

1. **Referral loop.** Every customer has a code (**Profile → Invite friends**).
   - Reward suggestion: **₹100 to the referrer and ₹100 off for the friend** after the friend's first delivered order.
   - The app doesn't hold money, so the team pays the referrer weekly by UPI using **Admin → Content → Referrals**. The friend's ₹100 off is a coupon the shop honours and Gadget Galli reimburses.
   - Cap it at ₹1,000 per referrer per month to prevent abuse.
2. **Share loop.** Every product and shop has a **share link** that opens the app (or the store). Encourage "send this to the person you're buying for" in videos and messages.
3. **Shop loop.** More shops lead to fewer zero-result searches, higher conversion, more customers and more shop interest. Run it weekly: take the top 20 **searches with no results**, list the shops that could stock them, and call them.
4. **Demand loop for shops.** **Demand near you** shows each shop what to stock. Shops that add those items get orders, which makes them evangelists.
5. **Review loop.** After delivery the app asks for a rating. Ask shops to remind customers on WhatsApp ("please rate us on Gadget Galli 🙏"). More reviews bring more trust and better conversion.

## 6. Converting visits into orders

**Listing quality (coach every shop):**
- Real photos (3+), the correct variant (storage/colour) and **updated prices**. Ask for a weekly price update; the one-tap price edit makes it about 2 minutes.
- Accurate stock. Use the **In/Out switch** or quantity tracking.
- Clear warranty (brand or shop) and installation charge where relevant.

**Pricing guidance for shops:**
- You don't need to be the cheapest. You need to be **fair and fast**. Within about 3–5% of online, plus same-day delivery, plus local warranty, wins.
- Bundle instead of discounting (case + screen guard; kit + installation).
- **Store pickup** with a small discount converts customers who live nearby.

**Speed and service promises:**
- Confirm in under 5 minutes. Dispatch within 2 hours of payment. Keep the "usually delivers in" time honest (the app calculates it from real orders).
- Use the **customer note** (e.g. "need GST bill") and call back if anything changes.

**Trust signals that convert:** Verified badge, ratings, "pay only after the shop confirms", the shop's address and photos, and fast replies to reviews.

## 7. Retention: the second order matters most

- **Cross-sell after delivery:** phone, then case, screen guard and charger. CCTV, then HDD upgrade and extra camera. PC part, then the next upgrade.
- **Push campaigns:** 1–2 per week maximum, always useful (price drops, new stock, festival deals). Use **Admin → Content → Push campaigns**, segmented by area.
- **Favourite shops:** remind customers to tap ♥ so the shop is one tap away next time.
- **Warranty and service:** "Problem with your item? Your shop is 3 km away." Local service is the biggest advantage over online. Say it often.
- **Problem resolution under 24 hours** (**Admin → Problems**). One well-handled complaint creates a loyal customer and a good review.

## 8. Money: pricing and monetisation roadmap

**Months 0–6: free for shops.** Build density and habits. The schema already supports featured placement, so paid plans can be switched on without app changes.

**From month 6, recommended:** a **simple fixed monthly plan**. Indian SMB owners prefer predictable costs to commissions, and it fits the "we never touch your money" promise.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Free forever | Listing, orders, reviews, QR poster |
| **Pro** | ₹999–1,499 / month | Featured in search and home for its categories, priority support, advanced insights, up to 3 staff logins (future) |
| Premium | ₹2,999 / month | Pro, plus home-banner slots in its zone, a monthly content shoot (a Reel featuring the shop) and a CCTV/installation lead priority |

**Other revenue:**
- **Brand and distributor sponsorships:** banners and "featured product" for Hikvision, CP Plus, Zotac, MSI and phone distributors, at ₹10,000–50,000 per month per slot.
- **Installation leads:** CCTV and PC build leads to shops at a fixed fee per qualified lead.
- **Delivery partnerships:** negotiate business rates with Porter or Rapido for shops (a perk worth paying for).

**Simple math:** 200 shops × 40% on Pro × ₹1,299 is about **₹1.04 lakh MRR**. Add 4 sponsor slots × ₹25,000 for **₹2 lakh+ per month** within year one in one city. Expansion to more cities multiplies it.

## 10. The first 90 days

**Weeks 1–2: pre-launch (supply)**
- Sign 25+ shops in Kukatpally/KPHB, CTC and Madhapur. List 1,500 products.
- Recruit 100 beta customers (friends, family, colleagues). Fix every bug and complaint within 48 hours. They and your first shops can double as the Google Play closed-test group (see SETUP.md, "Start these on day one").
- Shoot 15 videos (shop stories, price checks, delivery tests) so you launch with a content bank.

**Weeks 3–4: soft launch in West Hyderabad**
- Shops broadcast to their customers (§4.1). Post 5 Reels a week. Start Google Ads at ₹500 per day.
- Goal: **100 delivered orders**, confirm time under 10 minutes, rating at least 4.3.

**Days 31–60: expand**
- Add Ameerpet, Dilsukhnagar and Koti/Abids (75 shops). Launch the referral reward and campus ambassadors.
- Start Meta ads around live clusters. Approach 30 apartment associations for CCTV.
- Goal: **400 delivered orders per month**, 25% of customers ordering again within 30 days.

**Days 61–90: city-wide plus festival push**
- All zones covered (150 shops). Run the festival campaign (§9), PR story and auto-rickshaw ads in two clusters.
- Goal: **1,000 delivered orders per month**, under 15% of searches with no results, under 2% of orders with problems.

## 12. Team and weekly rhythm

**Roles (first 6 months):**
- Founder: sales, partnerships, PR.
- 2 field sales: shop onboarding and handholding.
- 1 community & content lead: WhatsApp groups, Reels, ambassadors.
- 1 support & operations person: works from the admin panel.

**Daily (support):**
- **Admin → Dashboard**: clear the stuck orders and open problems.
- Approve new shops and custom products within 24 hours.

**Weekly (Monday, 45 minutes):**
1. North star: delivered orders this week vs last week.
2. Funnel (§13): find the biggest drop and pick one fix.
3. Top 20 **searches with no results**: who will call which shops?
4. Shop health: slowest 10 confirm times and the lowest ratings. Coach them or act.
5. Channel spend vs cost per first order: move budget.
6. Content plan for next week (5 videos, 2 push campaigns at most).

## 16. Protect the brand: trust, safety and compliance

- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.**
  - The rules require marketplaces to show seller details (the shop page shows name, address and contact) and to appoint a **Grievance Officer** whose name and contact appear in the app (add them to Terms in Help).
  - Acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month. Aim for 24 hours.
- **No fake MRPs or fake discounts.** Spot-check listings weekly. Shops that inflate MRP get a warning.
- **Verified badge = visit + documents.** Never sell or "fast-track" it.
- **Payment disputes:** Gadget Galli doesn't hold money, so your role is mediation. Get both sides on a call, collect the proofs (payment screenshot, package and bill photos are in the order) and record the outcome in **Admin → Problems**. Warn or suspend shops that don't cooperate.
- **SMS/DLT compliance** for OTPs, and **privacy:** the customer's phone number is visible to the shop only during an active order. Mention this in marketing; it's a real differentiator.
